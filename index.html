A robot car that finds fire before it spreads.

A two-ESP32 robot car you drive from a handheld remote. It detects flames, stops for obstacles, warns you with LEDs and a buzzer, runs a small water pump, and reports its status to an online dashboard.

Read the project Get in touch

What the robot does

Flame detection

A flame sensor detects visible flames. Red, green and yellow LEDs and a buzzer show normal, fire and obstacle conditions.

Remote control

A handheld ESP32 with five buttons (Forward, Backward, Left, Right, Stop) drives the robot over ESP-NOW. No router or internet needed.

Obstacle stop

An ultrasonic sensor measures distance and stops the robot before a collision. This overrides movement commands.

IoT monitoring

Flame status, distance, alarm, pump and robot status appear on an Adafruit IO dashboard when Wi-Fi is available.

Water pump

A small pump, switched by a protected driver circuit, demonstrates water delivery in a controlled test.

How it works

The handheld ESP32 sends commands. The robot ESP32 reads the sensors, drives the motors and pump, and sends status online.

  1. The operator presses a button on the handheld remote.
  2. ESP-NOW carries the command to the robot, which drives the motors.
  3. Sensors check for flames and obstacles. The robot stops for obstacles and sounds an alert for fire.
  4. Status is sent to Adafruit IO over Wi-Fi when online.
